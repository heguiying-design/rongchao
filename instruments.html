Instruments

The in-house toolkit.

Edinburgh FLS1000

Photoluminescence spectrometer — steady-state and time-resolved emission, including the NIR region central to our APNC work.

Shimadzu UV-3600

UV–vis–NIR spectrophotometer — absorption fingerprinting of atomically precise nanoclusters across the full optical window.

Ultrafast Systems TA setup

Femtosecond pump–probe transient absorption — real-time excited-state dynamics of nanoclusters and quantum rods.

Optistat CF cryostat

Continuous-flow cryostat — temperature-dependent optical spectroscopy from cryogenic to ambient conditions.
